karga.VERIFICATION & STELLAR

Keep points private. Make rewards traceable.

Earned actions.
A separate, visible payment.

100 verified KP → 1 Testnet XLM

A reusable cup earns 25 KP, a bottle refill 30 KP, and greener transport 50 KP after organizer verification. Each action counts once.

  • Points, accounts and private journey records stay in PostgreSQL. No NFTs or collectible tokens.
  • Wallet activation funds 2 Testnet XLM; it is separate from the earned 1 XLM reward.
  • Persisted signed transactions are reused on retries to avoid duplicate payments. Keys stay server-side and encrypted at rest.
  • The organizer dashboard reviews participants, verification requests and payment status. It does not publish exact route labels.

Testnet XLM has no monetary value. An actual successful earned-payment demonstration is still unverified in this draft; show a real confirmation only if available.

Implemented payment integration is not evidence of a completed live payout.04 / 07
