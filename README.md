# Karga
Small Actions. Collective Impact.

Karga is a climate action platform that makes sustainable living measurable, engaging, and rewarding.

Karga helps individuals understand their carbon footprint, make more sustainable choices, earn rewards, and contribute to collective climate impact.

## Our Solution

Karga transforms everyday sustainable choices into measurable actions and meaningful rewards.

Users can:

* 🌱 Track: Understand their carbon footprint.
* 🚶 Act: Discover and adopt sustainable behaviors.
* ⭐ Earn: Collect points for eligible climate-friendly actions.
* 🏆 Compete: Participate in challenges and leaderboards.
* 🌎 Impact: Contribute to collective sustainability goals.

Track → Act → Earn → Impact

Our goal is to make climate action part of everyday life.


## Key Features

🌍 Carbon Footprint Tracking

Estimate carbon emissions from activities such as flights and transportation.

⭐ Karga Points

A gamified reward system designed to encourage sustainable behavior.

🏆 Community Leaderboard

Encourage participation through friendly competition and collective challenges.

📊 Personal Climate Dashboard

Help users understand their environmental impact and track progress over time.

🌱 Collective Climate Impact

Bring individual actions together to visualize community-level progress.

Feature availability depends on the current MVP implementation. Some features may be prototypes or planned functionality.

## 🔗 Why Stellar?

Karga explores how blockchain infrastructure can make climate rewards more transparent and useful.

The Stellar ecosystem provides opportunities for:

1. Digital Rewards

Enable users to receive and manage eligible rewards through Stellar wallets.

2. Transparent Transactions

Record reward transactions on-chain, creating a transparent transaction history.

3. Climate Contributions

Explore integration with Stellarcarbon to allow users to support verified carbon-credit retirement projects.

Why Blockchain?

We believe sustainable actions should create value beyond traditional application points.

Stellar can help connect climate engagement with transparent digital rewards and verifiable contributions.

Note: Planned integrations are not presented as completed features. On-chain reward functionality and Stellarcarbon integration should be assessed against the current implementation.
